# Week 2 Tutorial 2

## Scenario

A person can enter the movie theater if:

- Age is 13 or above
- OR the person is with an adult
- AND the person has a valid ticket

## 1. Components

### Inputs

- Age
- With adult
- Valid ticket

### Process

Check the age, adult companion, and ticket.

### Output

- Allowed to enter
- Not allowed to enter
